Fluoro station: bench activity summary

Activity summary

Students prepare and image fixed and live cells on an EVOS M5000 fluorescence microscope to find the nucleus, cytoskeleton and mitochondria for their scavenger hunt. This version uses three cell types: SF21 insect cells, mouse embryonic fibroblasts (MEFs) and Paramecium. This version uses two cell types: SF21 insect cells and mouse embryonic fibroblasts (MEFs). This version uses one cell type, mouse embryonic fibroblasts (MEFs), in two conditions: healthy and diseased. Students compare the two.

Samples

  • SF21: insect (moth ovary) cells, fixed, grown directly on the base of the well
  • MEFs: mouse embryonic fibroblasts, fixed, on round coverslips
  • Healthy MEFs: mouse embryonic fibroblasts, fixed, on round coverslips
  • Diseased MEFs: the same cells under the disease condition, fixed, on round coverslips. Disease model: TBC
  • Live Paramecium: free-swimming in culture medium
  • Fixed Paramecium: fixed onto poly-L-lysine coated coverslips

How samples are presented

  • One 6-well plate per bench side. All 6 wells are used, with no duplicate wells.4 wells are used. Column 3 is left empty.
  • The lid is labelled with the sample name and colour stickers showing which stain each well gets
  • SF21 and MEF wells use the same addition method, so one protocol covers both
  • Column 1 is healthy and column 2 is diseased. Both get exactly the same stains, so the condition is the only difference.
  • The DAPI/FITC wells (row A) are MitoTracker Red pre-stained before fixing, shown by a dashed red ring on the lid

Imaging

  • The whole plate goes into the EVOS M5000 caddy, so no slides are needed
  • Fluorescence channels: DAPI (blue), GFP (green, for FITC) and RFP (red, for MitoTracker)
  • Methylene blue wells are imaged in transmitted light. They can also be explored on the fluoro scope if students are interested.
  • Images are uploaded to the iPad workbook

How the station runs

Welcome screen→ Worked example: methylene blue on healthy MEFs ★→ Choose 1–2 more wells→ Image on EVOS→ Record in workbook
  • Each student or pair prepares 2 of the 64 wells because of time limits. Between them the side completes the whole plate, so there is plenty to see.
  • Everyone can image every well on the plate, including wells other people prepared.
  • Group size per side: TBC. Time at station: TBC.

Equipment per bench side

  • EVOS M5000 fluorescence microscope with plate caddy
  • iPad (protocols + workbook web app)
  • P20 and P200 pipettes on a rack, with a box of tips
  • Stain rack: methylene blue, DAPI/phalloidin-FITC mix, MitoTracker Red
  • 50 mL tube of PBS and a waste tube
  • 6-well plate of samples (labelled lid)
  • Shared: used-tip bucket and a dark box for incubations
  • Nearby: big-screen signpost, stereo microscope with live Paramecium, compound microscope with unstained MEFs
