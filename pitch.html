Part 1 · 90 minutes

Identify the cells

  • Samples A–E could be MEFs, Sf9 insect cells, Paramecium, E. coli or baker's yeast.
  • Groups move freely between six stations and record evidence on iPads.
  • They move on when they can convince an education officer.

Part 2 · 50 minutes

Test the cells

  • Each group picks one research project linked to a real scientist.
  • They design the experiment on an iPad and run a real plate on the FLUOstar.
  • Supporting images come from the EVOS, the SEM and a 3D cell model.
  • The day ends with a 2-minute pitch per group.

Why it works

  • Real gear: EVOS, SEM and FLUOstar, used by students.
  • Real people: a scientist coaches each group.
  • Less paper: one digital logbook, sent to the teacher as a PDF.
